# 📊 Dynamic Pricing Intelligence System

> A machine learning-powered retail analytics and pricing intelligence system for sales prediction, promotion analysis, store performance evaluation, and simulated pricing recommendations.

## 🚀 Overview

The **Dynamic Pricing Intelligence System** combines data analysis, machine learning, pricing simulation, and an interactive Streamlit chatbot to generate data-driven retail business insights.

The system can:

- Analyze store sales and customer behavior
- Identify high-performing stores
- Evaluate promotion effectiveness
- Predict store sales using Machine Learning
- Simulate different pricing scenarios
- Recommend a pricing scenario based on simulated revenue
- Answer business questions through an interactive chatbot

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Sales Analytics
Analyze total sales, average sales, customer activity, and store-level performance.

### 🤖 Machine Learning
Compare regression models and select the best-performing model for sales prediction.

### 📈 Promotion Analysis
Compare average sales during promotion and non-promotion periods.

### 💰 Pricing Simulation
Evaluate:
- 10% Lower Price
- Current Price
- 10% Higher Price

The system compares predicted demand and simulated revenue for each scenario.

### 💬 Interactive Pricing Chatbot

The project includes a **Streamlit-based Dynamic Pricing Intelligence Assistant** that allows users to interact with the system using natural-language questions.

Users can ask questions such as:

```text
What are the sales of Store 10?

How many customers did Store 10 have?

Give me a summary of Store 10.

Which store has the highest sales?

Do promotions increase sales?

Predict sales for Store 10.

Recommend a price for Store 10 at ₹100.
